## Primary scientific contrast

Formal confirmation should preserve the study's central separation. A candidate should not be confirmed merely because it has high empirical comeback frequency.

The formal rule should require evidence that, relative to a frozen within-root reference-best comparator, the candidate exhibits the prospectively specified practical-comeback/error-dependence structure while respecting its frozen strongest-policy quality/optimality-gap condition.

A positive result authorizes only a policy-conditioned statement. It does not establish objective superiority or game-theoretic optimality.

## Statistical unit

Root is the primary pairing/clustering unit. Multiple legal moves and continuation replicates from one root remain dependent observations.

The formal analysis must use a predeclared root-paired or root-clustered procedure. Increasing replicate count cannot repair a failed minimum-root-support gate.

## Multiplicity

If multiple candidates and/or multiple co-primary endpoints are formally tested, the family and correction method must be frozen before Stage 2 outcomes. No post-outcome selection of the most favorable candidate, phase, endpoint, or opponent strength is allowed.

## No-rescue rule

After Stage 2 outcome inspection, do not:

- add games beyond the frozen stopping/resource rule to pass support;
- relax confirmation floors;
- change root disadvantage definition;
- change successful-defense definition;
- choose a different opponent strength because it gives a favorable result;
- drop an unfavorable phase unless that rule was already frozen;
- add features or redefine the candidate;
- replace the primary endpoint;
- reuse Stage 1 evidence;
- reinterpret `NON-ESTIMABLE` or `RESOURCE-CENSORED` as `NOT-CONFIRMED`.

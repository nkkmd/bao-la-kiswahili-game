## 1. Primary scientific question

> Can Bao contain position-transferrable move-pattern families that recur across independent trajectories and fresh rule states, and that reproducibly combine a frozen deeper-search decision loss with a consistent structural/reply/misvaluation failure signature?

This question concerns a frozen machine operationalization, not game-theoretic optimality or human cognition.

## 2. Construct-level hypotheses

### BMP-H1 — Machine-reproducible decision-loss pattern existence

At least one Stage 1-promoted candidate may reproduce on fresh Stage 2 roots such that the candidate move is systematically worse than same-root alternatives under the frozen D3+Q1 exact-search reference.

This is not assumed true. Zero confirmed candidates is valid.

### BMP-H2 — Structural / reply failure consistency

For any candidate promoted with a structural or forcing-response failure definition, the frozen failure signature may recur on fresh Stage 2 roots at a rate exceeding its preregistered benchmark and minimum observed-rate gate.

The exact benchmark/threshold is not frozen at Study initiation and must be fixed before Stage 2 generation.

### BMP-H3 — Shallow/deeper search misvaluation family

A subset of candidates may satisfy a prospectively defined shallow-attractiveness condition at D1/D2 while becoming clearly inferior under D3+Q1.

D2 is a comparator, not the primary reference. D2→D3 disagreement cannot be used to change the chosen reference depth after data inspection.

### BMP-H4 — Static evaluator misvaluation family

A subset of candidates may appear favorable under a static post-move `bao` evaluation yet be inferior under the D3+Q1 root-candidate reference.

Static score is not a probability and will not be calibrated into a formal win-probability loss using Calibration Study 1.

### BMP-H5 — Outcome consequence as secondary evidence

For candidate families authorized for fresh deterministic continuation, the candidate branch may have worse population-level empirical outcomes than a prospectively frozen same-root reference branch under the same continuation policy.

This outcome is policy-conditional empirical evidence, not intrinsic or game-theoretic win probability.

## First B1-B2 conclusion

The profile ordering is not explained by candidate availability alone.

In all three comparisons the condition with the higher formal expansion-game rate has both:

1. more candidate-bearing games; and
2. a substantially higher probability that a candidate-bearing game manifests at least one capture-branch-expansion.

The conditional-manifestation contrast is the larger multiplicative component, especially in both independent D3 corpora.

This is a strong Stage B constraint: the mechanism analysis must explain not only access to candidate positions, but why candidates generated by one profile are structurally much more expansion-compatible.

Therefore the D3 reversal is not merely dilution by P2 candidates outside forced-capture regimes.

## D2 versus D3 structural synthesis

The most informative cross-depth observation is that several **sustained forcing morphology** variables track the profile with the higher expansion-game rate in all three comparisons.

### E-018 D2, where P2 is favored

P2 versus LG:

- mean regime length: `43.06 > 25.51`
- normalized position: `0.397 < 0.603` — P2 candidates are earlier in the regime
- post-capture max: `7.60 > 6.70`
- post-persistence fraction: `0.584 > 0.363`
- recovery distance: `12.06 > 4.74`
- distance to forcing release: `25.91 > 10.43`

### E-019/E-020 D3, where LG is favored

The same six properties reverse with the formal event-rate ordering and favor LG in both independent D3 corpora:

- longer regimes;
- earlier normalized regime position;
- higher post-capture maximum;
- greater post-capture persistence;
- longer recovery distance;
- longer time until forcing release.

### Instantaneous branch amplitude is not sufficient

Two instantaneous candidate metrics do **not** track the favored profile across depths.

At E-018 D2:

- candidate capture-move count: P2 `6.27` < LG `6.94`
- capture delta: P2 `2.91` < LG `4.68`

Yet P2 has the much higher expansion-game rate.

Therefore a simple mechanism of the form:

> whichever profile produces the larger instantaneous capture branching at the candidate wins

is contradicted by the D2 secondary morphology.

The current data instead support prioritizing a mechanism based on **sustained forcing structure**: long forced-capture regimes, early candidate placement within the regime, high post-candidate branch ceiling, and persistence before release/recovery.

This remains a working mechanism interpretation, not a causal or formal claim.

## Expansion phenotype stability

Expansion-classified candidates across conditions generally occupy the high-branch, persistent subset of each candidate pool:

- candidate capture-move count is typically around 7-8 or higher;
- capture delta is strongly positive;
- post-persistence is at least the classification threshold by construction and is usually substantially above it;
- expansions tend to occur earlier than the overall candidate pool within long regimes.

This suggests a useful Stage B distinction:

1. **candidate-pool generation** — which structural candidate contexts each search profile enters;
2. **expansion-compatible morphology** — whether those candidates lie in sustained forced-capture contexts capable of satisfying the expansion definition.

The present summary does not yet establish whether profile differences remain after conditioning on phase or forced-capture membership. That requires the next existing-corpus subgroup reanalysis.

## Current mechanism working model

The strongest current non-formal working model is:

> Search profile and depth alter the morphology of the candidate pool. The profile that more often enters long, early-stage, persistent forced-capture regimes has a much higher probability that a detected category-A candidate manifests as capture-branch-expansion. The D2/D3 ordering reversal may therefore arise from a reversal in which profile preferentially reaches this sustained-forcing candidate regime, rather than from candidate count or instantaneous branching magnitude alone.

Interpretation limits:

- this is retrospective and descriptive;
- D2 and D3 corpora are not paired across depth;
- no causal mediation is established;
- no general search-profile × depth interaction is confirmed;
- no search-tree mechanism is directly observed.

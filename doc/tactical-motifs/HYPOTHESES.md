# HYPOTHESES — Tactical Motifs / Tesuji Study 1

## Status convention

All hypotheses below are **prospective study hypotheses**, not confirmed results. Stage 0 contains no scientific hypothesis test.

## TM-H1 — Recurrent structural move motifs exist

Across fresh, non-duplicate Bao rule states from independent historical trajectories, there exist recurring combinations of:

`structural precondition → move family → reproducible state transformation`

that occur more broadly than one exact state or one opening sequence.

**Current status:** NOT TESTED.

## TM-H2 — Some recurrent motifs are position-transferrable

At least one Stage 1 motif candidate can satisfy prospectively frozen transferability gates across multiple historical trajectories and non-identical rule states without being dominated by one opening prefix/family.

**Current status:** NOT TESTED.

## TM-H3 — Some transferrable motifs retain bounded strategic benefit

For at least one frozen Stage 2 candidate, the candidate move family produces the preregistered strategic/structural benefit relative to its comparator on a fresh independent corpus under the prospectively frozen decision rule.

This is the hypothesis that could support the term **confirmed tesuji** within the machine-reproducible scope of this study.

**Current status:** NOT AUTHORIZED FOR TESTING.

## Non-hypotheses / forbidden shortcuts

The following statements are not hypotheses automatically inherited from previous studies:

- `capture-branch-expansion` is a tesuji.
- MTAJI-M1 or MTAJI-M2 is a tesuji.
- N-ACT/N-CON defines tesuji classes.
- search instability or decision ambiguity identifies a tesuji.
- an AI best move is a tesuji.
- a frequent move is a tesuji.
- a joseki move is a tesuji.

Each would require an independent operational matching rule and fresh evidence.

## Candidate families for hypothesis generation only

The following are examples that Stage 1 may discover, reject, split, merge, or replace:

- delayed capture for downstream gain
- deliberate relay-ending/landing alteration
- forcing an opponent capture to damage structure
- temporary nyumba sacrifice for another benefit
- reserve placement that creates a multi-move plan
- front-row sacrifice that restores mobility

No candidate family is privileged by name. Failed exploratory families will remain failed/exploratory; renaming or threshold adjustment cannot convert them into Stage 2 candidates without the frozen promotion rule.

## Null outcomes

A scientifically valid closure can be:

- no recurrent motif family meets Stage 1 promotion gates;
- recurrence exists but transferability fails;
- a candidate is estimable but Stage 2 `not-confirmed`;
- Stage 2 is technically inconclusive;
- the available representation is insufficient for a defensible tesuji claim.

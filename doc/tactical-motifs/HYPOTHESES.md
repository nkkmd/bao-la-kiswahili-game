# HYPOTHESES — Tactical Motifs / Tesuji Study 1

Updated: 2026-08-15  
Status: **STUDY 1 CLOSED / FINAL HYPOTHESIS DECISIONS**

This document preserves the prospectively defined Study 1 hypotheses and records their final resolution. Stage 0 contained no scientific hypothesis test. Stage 1 was exploratory; Stage 2 supplied the fresh prospective formal confirmation layer.

## TM-H1 — Recurrent structural move motifs exist

Across fresh, non-duplicate Bao rule states from independent historical trajectories, there exist recurring combinations of:

`structural precondition → move family → reproducible state transformation`

that occur more broadly than one exact state or one opening sequence.

**Final status: SUPPORTED / RESOLVED POSITIVELY AT THE EXPLORATORY-STRUCTURAL LEVEL.**

Stage 1 measured 715 unique rule states and 3,148 exact legal move records, enumerated 3,116,520 raw tactical-pattern instances, and found 948 detailed candidates that passed every frozen promotion gate. Eight definitions were promoted by the preregistered deterministic ranking/caps.

This is not a separate Stage 2 multiplicity-controlled candidate decision; it establishes that recurrent structural motif candidates existed under the frozen Stage 1 representation.

## TM-H2 — Some recurrent motifs are position-transferrable

At least one Stage 1 motif candidate can satisfy prospectively frozen transferability gates across multiple historical trajectories and non-identical rule states without being dominated by one opening prefix/family.

**Final status: SUPPORTED / RESOLVED POSITIVELY.**

In fresh Stage 2 data, all four canonical formal candidates passed all six frozen estimability/transferability gates. The smallest selected formal set still contained 1,031 unique historical trajectories / rule states, 891 distinct opening prefixes, and all six generation strata.

Passing these gates does not by itself imply tactical-value confirmation; C01, C02, and C04 remained `NOT-CONFIRMED` after co-primary endpoint evaluation.

## TM-H3 — Some transferrable motifs retain bounded strategic benefit

For at least one frozen Stage 2 candidate, the candidate move family produces the preregistered strategic/structural benefit relative to its comparator on a fresh independent corpus under the prospectively frozen decision rule.

**Final status: CONFIRMED FOR AT LEAST ONE CANDIDATE — `TM-S2-C03`.**

`TM-S2-C03` passed all frozen requirements:

```text
selected roots = 1272
structural success = 1245 / 1272 = 0.978774
D3 top-set success = 937 / 1272 = 0.736635
D3 at-or-above-state-median = 0.869497
D3 unique-worst = 0.070755
formal decision = CONFIRMED
```

The other canonical candidates were formally resolved as:

```text
TM-S2-C01 = NOT-CONFIRMED
TM-S2-C02 = NOT-CONFIRMED
TM-S2-C04 = NOT-CONFIRMED
```

Accordingly, the existential TM-H3 statement is confirmed within the machine-reproducible scope of the frozen Bao engine/search operationalization. This does **not** establish traditional/expert-recognized tesuji, human importance, beginner importance, pedagogical value, causal strategic benefit beyond the formal construct, or external validity across other engines/rules/search instruments.

## Non-hypotheses / forbidden shortcuts

The following statements were not hypotheses automatically inherited from previous studies and remain unsupported by this Study 1 unless separately operationalized:

- `capture-branch-expansion` is a tesuji.
- MTAJI-M1 or MTAJI-M2 is a tesuji.
- N-ACT/N-CON defines tesuji classes.
- search instability or decision ambiguity identifies a tesuji.
- an AI best move is a tesuji.
- a frequent move is a tesuji.
- a joseki move is a tesuji.

Each would require an independent operational matching rule and fresh evidence.

## Candidate-family examples from study initiation

The following were hypothesis-generating examples only, not a privileged ontology:

- delayed capture for downstream gain
- deliberate relay-ending/landing alteration
- forcing an opponent capture to damage structure
- temporary nyumba sacrifice for another benefit

The completed study did not retroactively rename Stage 1 candidates to match these examples and did not rescue failed formal candidates by relabeling.

## Final closure

Study 1 closes with:

- recurrent structural motif candidates observed in Stage 1;
- transferability/estimability demonstrated for all four canonical Stage 2 candidates;
- exactly one formally confirmed machine-reproducible transferable tactical motif (`TM-S2-C03`);
- three formally `NOT-CONFIRMED` candidates retained without rescue;
- human/expert/traditional/pedagogical validation deferred to a separate prospective study.

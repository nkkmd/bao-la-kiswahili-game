# HYPOTHESES — Critical Positions / Outcome Branching Study 1

Updated: 2026-08-23

## Construct hierarchy

Study 1 does not begin with a single omnibus criticality score. It separates outcome, search, stability and structure.

## CPOB-H1 — Primary exploratory existence question

**Question:** Under one frozen stochastic continuation policy, do fresh Bao roots exist for which exact legal-move intervention produces materially large root-actor empirical continuation outcome divergence?

Primary root measurement candidate:

```text
D_range(s) = max_m p_hat(s,m) - min_m p_hat(s,m)
```

Status: **CONSTRUCT FROZEN / NUMERIC CRITICALITY FLOOR PROVISIONAL UNTIL STAGE 0 CLOSE**

Boundary: `D_range` is policy-conditioned and empirical. It is not a game-theoretic move-value range.

## CPOB-H2 — Structural recurrence question

**Question:** Can high-divergence roots be represented by recurring, pre-root structural matchers that transfer across independent historical trajectories, opening prefixes and generation strata?

Status: **PLANNED STAGE 1 DISCOVERY → POSSIBLE STAGE 2 CONFIRMATION**

Candidate matchers cannot contain the divergence outcome itself.

## CPOB-H3 — Search/outcome separation

**Question:** How strongly does fixed D3 search-value separation align with empirical continuation divergence under the frozen policy?

Status: **SECONDARY / DESCRIPTIVE-EXPLORATORY IN STUDY 1 UNLESS SEPARATELY PREREGISTERED**

No score-to-win-probability conversion is allowed.

## CPOB-H4 — Ranking-instability relation

**Question:** Are high-divergence roots more likely to show D2→D3 tie-aware TopSet instability?

Status: **SECONDARY / HYPOTHESIS-GENERATING**

Position Complexity Study 1 remains `INCONCLUSIVE`; this question cannot retroactively confirm PCX-H1/H2.

## CPOB-H5 — Structural branch divergence

**Question:** Do high-divergence roots show larger between-move differences in immediate legal branching, capture availability, forcing/free-choice state, front-row structure, reserve/nyumba quantities or response envelopes?

Status: **SECONDARY / STRUCTURAL DECOMPOSITION**

Structural consequences do not become outcome probabilities.

## Explicit non-hypotheses

The following are not Study 1 hypotheses:

```text
engine score difference equals win-probability difference
this root has true winning probability X
a move is game-theoretically winning/losing
this is the unique theoretical turning point
humans perceive this root as important
experts recognize the root as a traditional turning point
BMP C01-C04 are validated critical-position markers
TM-S2-C03 is automatically a critical-position marker
```

These require different evidence or remain historical boundaries.

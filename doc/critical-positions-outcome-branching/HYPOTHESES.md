# HYPOTHESES — Critical Positions / Outcome Branching Study 1

Updated: 2026-08-24

## Construct hierarchy

Study 1 does not use a single omnibus criticality score. It separates outcome, search, stability and structure.

## CPOB-H1 — Primary exploratory existence question

**Question:** Under one frozen stochastic continuation policy, do fresh Bao roots exist for which exact legal-move intervention produces materially large root-actor empirical continuation outcome divergence?

Primary root measurement:

```text
D_range(s) = max_m p_hat(s,m) - min_m p_hat(s,m)
highDivergence = D_range >= 0.30
```

Observed Stage 1 exploratory result:

```text
Namua high-divergence roots = 52 / 300
Mtaji high-divergence roots = 87 / 300
overall = 139 / 600
```

Status: **EXPLORATORY EXISTENCE OBSERVED / NOT A FORMAL CONFIRMATION LABEL**

Boundary: `D_range` is policy-conditioned and empirical. It is not a game-theoretic move-value range or validated winning-probability difference.

## Explicit non-hypotheses / closure boundary

The following are not established by Study 1:

```text
engine score difference equals win-probability difference
this root has true winning probability X
a move is game-theoretically winning/losing
this is the unique theoretical turning point
humans perceive this root as important
experts recognize the root as a traditional turning point

no higher-dimensional or nonlinear critical-position representation exists
```

A future study may test a richer representation only as a new prospective independent design with fresh evidence; it may not rescue this Study by relaxing the frozen Stage 1 grammar or promotion thresholds.

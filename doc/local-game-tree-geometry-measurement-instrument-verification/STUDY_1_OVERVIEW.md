## Answer

**Yes, within the frozen bounded domain of this Study.**

**`FORMAL-ELIGIBLE-ALL`**

## Why a new Study was required

G3-01 (`LGTGMF-STUDY1`) is permanently technical-invalid. Because the mismatch was discovered after fresh evidence had been generated/read, the no-rescue rule prohibited same-evidence repair.

This Study started anew. It did not revise G3-01, reuse its scientific population, or inherit eligibility from its candidate families.

# Study 1 Overview

## Question

Can a bounded local Bao game-tree/RAW-graph geometry measurement instrument be prospectively defined so that its scientific canonical objects are deterministic, exact, traversal/order invariant and independently reproducible, while runtime/resource telemetry is kept completely outside the scientific digest?

## Why a new Study is required

G3-01 (`LGTGMF-STUDY1`) is permanently technical-invalid. Its production and independent implementations agreed on the selected roots and local geometry measurements, but the frozen stage-level canonical artifact mixed deterministic scientific content with implementation-dependent telemetry. Because the mismatch was discovered after fresh evidence had been generated/read, the no-rescue rule prohibited same-evidence repair.

This Study starts anew. It does not revise G3-01, reuse its scientific population, or inherit eligibility from its F1-F5 families.

## Prospective design

The Study separates three questions:

1. **Stage 0:** Does the instrument contract itself behave deterministically and reproducibly on synthetic/non-scientific controls?
2. **Stage 1:** On fresh development evidence, which prospectively frozen metric families satisfy exact production/independent reconstruction and resource/estimability gates?
3. **Stage 2:** On a separate fresh formal holdout, which Stage-1-promoted families remain exactly reproducible?

## Candidate measurement families

The new family IDs are:

- `LGTGMIV-F1-TREE-OCCURRENCE`
- `LGTGMIV-F2-RAW-GRAPH`
- `LGTGMIV-F3-TRANSPOSITION-RECONVERGENCE`
- `LGTGMIV-F4-TREE-GRAPH-RELATION`
- `LGTGMIV-F5-REPLY-GEOMETRY`

They cover the same broad geometry domains as the failed G3-01 candidate families, but their definitions and eligibility are newly frozen under this Study. No eligibility transfers from G3-01.

## Primary verification principle

The scientific object must be independent of execution environment. Therefore elapsed time, wall clock, RSS, CPU timing, runner identity, filesystem path, PID, job/workflow identity and upload timing are telemetry only. They may determine resource estimability but never enter a scientific canonical hash.

## Claim boundary

A positive result authorizes only the bounded RAW local-game-tree/graph measurement families that pass this Study's formal gates at relative depth 5 and within the frozen populations/resource ceilings. It does not authorize:

- whole-Bao state-space or game-tree size estimates
- symmetry or canonical state reduction
- strategic-regime representations
- causal or player-strength claims
- automatic G3-02 execution
- opening of the protected standard-root depth-10 exact holdout

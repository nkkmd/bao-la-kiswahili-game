# HYPOTHESES — Symmetry / Isomorphic Positions Study 1

These are prospective hypotheses, not findings.

## H1 — Local-coordinate seat swap

Swapping player-indexed state while preserving row, pit index, side and direction may define a rule-semantic isomorphism because pit coordinates are player-local.

## H2 — House-inactive Mtaji left/right reflection

When `phase=mtaji`, `reserve=[0,0]` and `houseOwned=[false,false]`, reversing each row's pit indices and flipping left/right move orientation may define an isomorphism. This scope is defined prospectively because the active nyumba coordinate `HOUSE=4` statically breaks a global `i -> 7-i` mapping.

## H3 — Composition

The composition of H1 and H2 may also be an isomorphism on their common domain.

## H4 — Phase / state-semantic dependence

A transform can be valid on a restricted Mtaji house-inactive domain while being invalid as an all-Bao transform. Such scope differences must be declared before formal outcomes.

## H5 — Reachability is separate

A rule-semantic isomorphism need not preserve reachability from the repository's fixed numeric-player standard initial state.

## H6 — Conditional canonicalization

Only transformations that pass all applicable exact gates may participate in a downstream canonicalization contract.

## Null/negative outcomes are acceptable

Zero validated scientific candidates is an admissible Study 1 outcome.

## Primary cohort inclusion

All conditions would have been required:

1. age `>=18` and valid informed consent;
2. self-reported Bao la Kiswahili playing experience `>=3 years`;
3. sufficient neutral rules/position competence on a C03-independent screening instrument;
4. qualification by either Route A or Route B below;
5. no prior exposure to the published C03 formal definition/result before completing the formal tasks;
6. no role in Tactical Motifs Study 1 candidate discovery/formal evaluation or this study's stimulus selection/coding.

## Route A — documented/community credential route

Requires `>=3 years` Bao experience plus at least one prospectively documented category:

- organized Bao competition/league participation;
- sustained Bao teaching/coaching;
- recognized Bao club/community/association role or endorsement;
- other documented high-level Bao practice accepted under a preregistered evidence category.

The criterion is the category/evidence, not whether the person supports C03.

## Route B — long-experience competence route

For experts without formal tournament/organizational credentials:

- Bao experience `>=5 years`; and
- neutral competence screen score `>=90%`.

The screen must not contain C03-like items, reusable-pit hints, rightward-takata prompts, or Study 1 terminology.

## Neutral competence screen

The planned screen would cover general Bao rule/state interpretation, legal move identification, capture obligation, Namua/Mtaji distinction, sowing direction/state reconstruction, and nyumba handling without testing the C03 motif.

No threshold could be changed after seeing formal participant outcomes. In the realized Study 1, no participant was screened and no outcome was observed.

## Prior C03 exposure

Participants reporting prior knowledge of the Study 1 C03 candidate/result would have been excluded from the primary confirmatory cohort because the target identity was already revealed.

If retained with consent, they could only have formed a separately labeled exploratory `known-exposure` cohort. No such cohort was recruited.

## Duplicate participants

A private linkage mechanism was planned to identify repeated participation. Only the first eligible completed formal session would have been retained for primary analysis. No linkage table was created because recruitment never started.

## Incomplete responses

Primary analysis required at least `10` usable primary blocks out of planned `12`.

No participant responses were collected, so this completion rule was never applied to an individual.

## Forbidden post-hoc expert definition

The following remained prohibited:

- retaining only C03-supportive participants;
- reclassifying novices as experts to reach sample size;
- lowering experience/screen thresholds because recruitment is difficult;
- excluding an expert because their explanation conflicts with C03;
- adding a new credential route based on observed outcomes.

The frozen minimum expert count was not reached and was not lowered. The final human-axis outcome is therefore `INCONCLUSIVE-NOT-ESTIMABLE (N=0)`.

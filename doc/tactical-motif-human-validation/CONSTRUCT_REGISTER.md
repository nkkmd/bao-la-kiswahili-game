# CONSTRUCT_REGISTER — Tactical Motif Human / Expert Validation Study 1

Updated: 2026-08-16

## Vocabulary firewall

### machine-confirmed tactical motif

A motif satisfying Tactical Motifs Study 1's frozen machine operationalization and formal decision rule. C03 already has this status. This is historical evidence.

### human-recognized tactical motif

A recurring cross-position move principle that qualified human participants can discriminate or group across non-identical positions under the present prospective instrument.

### expert-recognized tesuji

An explicit expert label/category judgment that the structure is appropriately described as a tesuji or equivalent reusable local tactical principle. This is not synonymous with the primary discrimination endpoint.

### traditional tesuji

A historically/transmission-based claim about established Bao knowledge. Requires literature/oral-history/ethnographic evidence and is out of scope here.

### pedagogically useful tesuji

A teaching/learning effectiveness claim. Requires separate learner outcomes and is out of scope here.

### best move / strong move

A position-specific value judgment. A move can be best/strong without forming a reusable motif.

### tactical sequence / forced sequence

A concrete response sequence property. It is not automatically a transferable motif.

### joseki

Opening-sequence knowledge. A pattern whose identity depends on a particular opening prefix is not treated as cross-position tesuji evidence here.

### general strategic principle

A broader strategic rule that may span more contexts than C03's local machine construct. C03 validation does not establish a universal principle.

## Endpoint hierarchy

### Primary

`crossPositionPrincipleDiscrimination`

Operational target: distinguish pairs of fresh C03-eligible positions sharing the frozen C03 structural/move principle from prospectively matched near-miss pairs, without opening history or C03 terminology.

### Secondary

- `moveChoiceRecognition`
- `explicitTesujiOrReusablePrincipleLabel`
- `transferabilityConfidence`

### Exploratory

- free-text ontology coding
- subgroup heterogeneity
- prior style/region/training descriptors if ethically collected and sample size permits
- C01/C02/C04 human calibration

Exploratory findings cannot rescue a failed primary result.

## Vocabulary firewall

### machine-confirmed tactical motif

A motif satisfying Tactical Motifs Study 1's frozen machine operationalization and formal decision rule. C03 already has this status. This is historical evidence and remains unchanged by the N=0 human closure.

### human-recognized tactical motif

A recurring cross-position move principle that qualified human participants can discriminate or group across non-identical positions under the prospective instrument.

This construct was not estimable in Study 1 because no qualified human participant responses were collected.

### expert-recognized tesuji

An explicit expert label/category judgment that the structure is appropriately described as a tesuji or equivalent reusable local tactical principle. This is not synonymous with the primary discrimination endpoint.

This construct was not evaluated in Study 1.

### traditional tesuji

A historically/transmission-based claim about established Bao knowledge. Requires literature/oral-history/ethnographic evidence and is out of scope here.

### pedagogically useful tesuji

A teaching/learning effectiveness claim. Requires separate learner outcomes and is out of scope here.

### best move / strong move

A position-specific value judgment. A move can be best/strong without forming a reusable motif.

### tactical sequence / forced sequence

A concrete response sequence property. It is not automatically a transferable motif.

### joseki

Opening-sequence knowledge. A pattern whose identity depends on a particular opening prefix is not treated as cross-position tesuji evidence here.

### general strategic principle

A broader strategic rule that may span more contexts than C03's local machine construct. C03 machine confirmation does not establish a universal principle.

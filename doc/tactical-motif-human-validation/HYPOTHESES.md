# HYPOTHESES — Tactical Motif Human / Expert Validation Study 1

Updated: 2026-08-16  
Status: **STAGE 0 PROSPECTIVE HYPOTHESES / NO HUMAN DATA**

## TMHV-H1 — Primary: cross-position transfer recognition

Bao experts exposed to blinded, position-only formal stimuli will identify C03-C03 cross-position pairs as sharing a reusable move-selection principle more consistently than expected under the participant-level null.

Planned participant-level quantity:

```text
primaryScore_i = correct primary blocks / usable primary blocks
participantSuccess_i = 1 iff primaryScore_i > 0.5
```

Formal Stage 2 hypothesis candidate:

```text
H0: P(participantSuccess=1) <= 0.5
H1: P(participantSuccess=1) > 0.5
```

This is a population-level expert-recognition hypothesis, not an independence assumption over individual position responses.

## TMHV-H2 — Secondary: move-choice recognition

On blinded individual C03-eligible positions not used in H1, experts will select a move matching the frozen C03 move abstraction at a rate materially above matched control expectations/chance defined prospectively in the Stage 2 spec.

This endpoint cannot substitute for H1 if H1 fails.

## TMHV-H3 — Secondary: explicit reusable-principle / tesuji labeling

After uncued primary and move-choice tasks are complete, experts will classify the C03 structure as a reusable local tactical principle or tesuji more often than as a position-specific best move, joseki-only move, or coincidental similarity.

Label wording and category merge rules must be frozen before formal responses.

## TMHV-H4 — Exploratory: explanation agreement

Expert explanations may contain concepts corresponding to the frozen machine construct, including low reusable-pit structure, rightward row-1 takata morphology, preservation/transformation of nyumba-related seed structure, or explicit cross-position transfer rationale.

Unless an independent blinded coding workflow is available and frozen before Stage 2, H4 remains exploratory and cannot alter the formal label.

## Valid negative results

All are scientifically valid:

- experts do not preferentially recognize C03 as a common principle;
- experts choose C03 moves but do not group them as one motif;
- experts recognize a common structure but reject the term `tesuji`;
- recognition is heterogeneous;
- machine-salient structure is not human-salient;
- experts identify a different principle;
- sample is not estimable.

None of these changes the historical machine decision `TM-S2-C03 = CONFIRMED`.

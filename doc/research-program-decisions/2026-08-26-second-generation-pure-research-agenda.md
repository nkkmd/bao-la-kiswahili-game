# Second-Generation Pure Research Agenda — Program Decision

Date: 2026-08-26
Status: ACTIVE PROGRAM DECISION
Scope: Bao research program governance

## Decision

The Bao second-generation research program is established as a **pure research track** and is scientifically/operationally separated from the public Bao AI improvement program.

The canonical agenda is `doc/FUTURE_RESEARCH_AGENDA.md` Version 2.0.0, Section 9.

## Fixed program boundaries

1. Generation 1 formal decisions and interpretation boundaries remain immutable.
2. `G2-01`..`G2-12` and `G2-H01` are agenda sequence labels, not formal Study IDs.
3. Every Generation 2 Study receives a new prospective contract and fresh scientific authorization before outcome generation.
4. Public-AI strength, win rate, deployment success, latency, UX, or product quality are not scientific endpoints for Generation 2.
5. Research results may later be inputs to a separate engineering program, but engineering outcomes do not change scientific decisions.
6. Research instruments are frozen per Study. Later public-AI changes do not retroactively change a Study's engine/evaluator/search semantics.
7. RAW state identity remains authoritative unless a new independent transformation-validation Study formally authorizes a transform.
8. Human claims require human evidence and remain non-blocking for the machine-only core program.

## Structural correction made during final agenda audit

The final pre-registration-level agenda review made three design corrections before synchronization:

- names such as “Study 2” were replaced by distinct new independent Study titles to avoid implying reopening/rescue of closed Generation 1 studies;
- RAW-only exact-oracle expansion was made independent of symmetry/canonicalization validation, so a non-confirmed symmetry Study cannot block valid RAW-only exact research;
- deeper bounded exact enumeration and full-game growth estimation were split into separate Studies, preventing post-outcome selection of an estimator from partial exact results.

These are prospective program-design corrections only. They do not modify any completed Study result.

## Program shape

```text
Generation 2 core = G2-01 .. G2-12
Human track = G2-H01 (independent / non-blocking)
AI improvement = separate engineering program, outside Generation 2 scientific endpoints
```

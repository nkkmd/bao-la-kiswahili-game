## Program position

`G2-02` is a Wave A / P0 measurement-foundation Study. It is independent of `G2-01 / PEOCR-STUDY1` and does not reopen any Research Generation 1 Study.

The primary construct is machine search reliability / decision robustness under prospectively frozen search-condition perturbations. It is explicitly distinct from human difficulty, structural complexity, empirical win probability, game-theoretic value, engine-evaluation correctness, public-AI strength, and human perception.

A higher-resource search condition may be used only as a frozen search reference. It is not game-theoretic truth, a validated optimal move, or a true-best-move oracle.

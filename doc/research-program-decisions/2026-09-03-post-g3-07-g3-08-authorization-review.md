## 1. Independent scientific meaning

**PASS.** G3-08 remains scientifically distinct from G3-07.

G3-07 asked whether prospectively frozen search-output changes were associated with bounded local geometry at selected roots. G3-08 instead asks how exact bounded geometry measured at one trajectory state relates to exact bounded geometry at later trajectory states, including lagged dependence, decay, reversal and first-exit behavior.

A valid G3-08 result may be positive, negative, null, non-estimable, inconclusive, or technical-invalid without changing any G3-07 decision.

## 2. Protection against post-G3-07 outcome selection

**PASS WITH MANDATORY OUTCOME-BLIND DESIGN.** The G3-08 question and candidate endpoint family were already recorded in the historical Research Generation 3 program plan before the G3-07 formal result.

G3-08 therefore may not use the three G3-07 `CONFIRMED` candidate identities, their thresholds, p-values, support margins, directions, search contrasts or search-output fields to select the G3-08 geometry panel, lags, persistence thresholds, endpoints, formal direction or support gates.

In particular, G3-08 is not authorized as a follow-up test of `root legal width × ranking-preorder change` and does not require any search-output endpoint at all.

## C. Rule-semantic event definition and identifiability

**PASS WITH MANDATORY RESTRICTIONS.** Current engine semantics permit prospective, deterministic event identity, but the event families do not all support the same control design.

The Study freeze must bind every event to:

```text
pre-root RAW identity
canonical legal move identity
complete post-move RAW identity
event label set
phase before / after
overlap / compound-event labels
source trajectory / source ply / event-window identity
```

At minimum the prospective event universe must address:

- capture-related move semantics;
- reserve-related semantics;
- nyumba ownership/use semantics;
- Namua→Mtaji transition semantics.

However, current rule semantics impose two important identifiability facts that must be preserved rather than hidden:

1. **Capture is mandatory.** When a capture is available at a root, the legal move set contains capture moves rather than a mixture of capture and non-capture moves. Therefore a generic `capture vs non-capture` same-root counterfactual contrast does not exist.
2. **Namua moves decrement reserve.** Generic reserve decrement is a phase-linked rule operation rather than a legal-move choice that varies within the same Namua root. Final reserve depletion and Namua→Mtaji transition are mechanically coupled in the current engine.

Consequences:

- generic capture and generic reserve effects may be studied only as event-conditioned / move-conditioned structural associations with prospectively frozen non-causal controls;
- any same-root contrast may be used only where legal move variants actually differ in the event of interest, for example eligible nyumba-loss/use contrasts;
- reserve depletion and Namua→Mtaji may be represented as an explicit compound/linked event rather than falsely treated as independent causal mechanisms;
- compound-event overlap must be recorded prospectively and may not be removed post hoc to improve a result;
- event rarity / non-comparability must lead to `NON-ESTIMABLE` or candidate non-promotion under frozen gates, not seed extension or favorable replacement.

## 3. Scientific construct and outcome firewall

The scientific question is whether a richer, prospectively frozen representation of information available at or before a selected root can reproducibly identify decision-critical structure in a fresh independent population.

The primary criticality construct remains a **fixed-policy empirical continuation-divergence construct**, not game-theoretic value, validated win probability, human criticality, or public-AI performance.

At study start, the prior Study's operational `D_range` definition and `D_range >= 0.30` high-divergence boundary are retained as the default construct continuity reference. G2-06 may not relax that boundary in response to G2-06 outcomes. Any replacement of the criticality construct before Stage 1 scientific outcome generation requires a new prospective freeze and explicit rationale; after Stage 1 outcome generation, construct substitution is prohibited for this Study.

Representation inputs are classified into exactly four leakage classes:

```text
A = PRE_ROOT_OBSERVABLE
B = ROOT_DERIVED_OUTCOME_INDEPENDENT
C = SEARCH_DERIVED_OUTCOME_INDEPENDENT
D = CONTINUATION_OR_FUTURE_OUTCOME_DERIVED
```

Only classes A-C may enter a predictor representation. Class D is forbidden.

Root-local deterministic legal-move or bounded reply expansion computed entirely from the root before the decision-criticality continuation is class B, provided it does not consume the continuation rollout, winner, `D_range`, future trajectory, or any post-root sampled continuation state.

Search-derived features are class C only when the complete search profile, depth, node/time budget, quiescence semantics, evaluator/profile, PRNG/seed semantics, tie-breaking, and source identities are frozen before the relevant scientific outcome is generated.

## 12. Interpretation boundary

Even a positive G2-06 result would establish only that the prospectively frozen representation/instrument/endpoint identifies the specified fixed-policy decision-critical construct within the frozen fresh population.

It would not by itself establish:

- a true/game-theoretic turning point;
- human-perceived criticality;
- a universal Bao critical-position taxonomy;
- a causal mechanism;
- public AI improvement;
- a full-game strategic solution.

A negative/null/inconclusive/non-estimable result would not establish that Bao has no important positions.

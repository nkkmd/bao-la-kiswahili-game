2. Game and AI Processing

Computer-opponent thinking, rule evaluation, and local two-player game progression all run within your browser. The game has no feature that sends board positions, moves, results, or AI decisions to a game server. It also does not use login accounts, online multiplayer, or advertising SDKs.

3. Information Stored in Your Browser

This site stores your selected game mode, AI difficulty, first/second-player choice, and sound setting in browser localStorage. Only when you press “Record AI move,” it also stores up to 50 records containing the most recent board position, AI move, and AI search statistics in localStorage. These records do not include free-form text, your name, a device identifier, or a save timestamp. You can delete them using “Delete records” on the game screen or your browser's site-data controls. The site does not automatically transmit these AI diagnostic records externally.

A Service Worker caches game files so the site can also start offline as a PWA.
